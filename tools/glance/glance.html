At a glance

What we built, what happened when people used it, and what we changed.

Built

A working kiosk at our exhibit

  • A knob and three sliders on an ESP32, 162 settings
  • Nine real 360-degree worlds on a Meta Quest 3
  • Thermal tickets that come alive in AR

Used

66 tickets in one day

  • Visitors at our exhibit on 30 September 2026, many in a VR headset for the first time
  • The printer’s log holds 66 tickets from that day
  • “It felt like playing, not choosing.”

Changed

Three fixes from watching them

  • People walked past the AR ticket: we added a line beside the printer
  • Long questions were slow in a dim room: cut to one-liners
  • Not everyone knew a “note”: plain families, like woody and fresh

Team of three: Utsav Sharma, Sijo Joseph and Rajat Chaple. Quotes paraphrased from testers at the exhibit.

At a glance

What I designed, what I decided, and what still needs testing.

Designed

One schedule, three surfaces

  • The Carekin Android app for the organiser and WhatsApp for the helper
  • A clickable Figma prototype and a 49-second walkthrough
  • Sole designer, two weeks

Decided

Four ideas let go, three tries at one screen

  • Rejected push-only alerts, a smart pillbox, a daily call and a shared spreadsheet
  • The “What changed” card: a table, then colour only, then one sentence, one photo and who has seen it

Not tested yet

No carers have used it yet

  • Ideas were judged against three principles, not with users
  • The test plan is ready: eight tasks, three roles, five people per role
  • Targets to beat: no dose given twice; every carer confirms a change within a day
